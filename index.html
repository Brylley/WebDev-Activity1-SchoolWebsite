DEPED CURRICULUMS

The Department of Education (DepEd) uses the MATATAG K-10 Curriculum as its primary educational framework to decongest lessons and focus on foundational skills

Junior High School (JHS) Curriculum (Grades 7 to 10)

Junior High School serves as the bridge between elementary foundations and specialized senior high tracks. It heavily utilizes a spiral progression approach—meaning students revisit core subjects every year, with topics getting progressively advanced rather than being taught all at once in a single year.

Senior High School (SHS) Curriculum (Grades 11 and 12)

The Senior High School curriculum spans Grades 11 and 12, dividing learning into core, applied, and specialized academic or technical-vocational tracks designed to prepare students directly for college, entrepreneurship, or employment.

Special Needs and Inclusive Education Curriculum

Governed heavily by inclusive education laws (such as Republic Act No. 11650) and specialized DepEd frameworks, education for Learners with Disabilities (LWDs) and children with special needs is adaptive rather than rigid.

Special Programs

Aside from the standard academic tracks, DepEd offers Special Curricular Programs (SCPs). These are designed for students with distinct talents, interests, or inclinations, usually replacing the standard Technology and Livelihood Education (TLE) slots with specialized, intensive electives.
